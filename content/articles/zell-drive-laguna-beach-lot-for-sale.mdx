Buildable land in Laguna Beach is exceptionally rare. Even more uncommon is finding a parcel where the years of painstaking preparation — the design reviews, the soils reports, the neighbor negotiations, the architectural vision — have already been done for you.

The property at 2840 Zell Drive is that uncommon thing.

<EditorialDivider />

## The Lot

Situated in the Top of the World neighborhood, this 5,033-square-foot flat lot carries a listing price of $2,495,000. Realtor Jennifer Halbert first listed the property in August 2021, and it sold the following month. The current owner then spent several years navigating Laguna's famously rigorous design-review process — a path that demands patience, local expertise, and no small amount of persistence.

That process is now complete. Design-review approval has been secured, and permits are estimated to arrive within three to four months.

> "This isn't just a plot of land. It comes with plans and guidance. Buyers are set up for a smooth build."
>
> — Jennifer Halbert, Realtor

What transfers with the sale is substantial: architectural plans by David Parker, a fire access plan, drainage plan, landscape plan, soils report, story poles, renderings, and utility connections for public water, sewer, gas, electric, and cable. The estimated build timeline from there is approximately 18 months.

<EditorialDivider />

## The Architecture

David Parker is one of Laguna's most recognized residential architects — his homes are identifiable throughout the city for their warmth, their organic sensibility, and their deep attentiveness to light. (We've written about his philosophy before: for Parker, architecture begins with listening to the client and the site in equal measure.)

For Zell Drive, his initial inspiration came from sketches made during a family vacation in Utah. The concept that emerged — a butterfly-shaped design — then had to be tested against the particular constraints of this lot: a small, tapered footprint, utility easements threading through the parcel, and the need to preserve sightlines for neighboring properties.

<PullQuote author="David Parker, Architect">
  We ultimately found a 'sweet spot' where the house is low enough to not block the neighbor's views, but still high enough for us to have a spectacular ocean view.
</PullQuote>

The result is a low-profile residence anchored by a dramatic two-story atrium, sweeping terraces, and extensive glass walls that dissolve the line between interior and exterior. The design is organized around natural light and the experience of living in the space — qualities that Parker considers the foundation of any good home.

> "Architecture is really about the experience of living in a home. We think about qualities like light, flow, outdoor connection, and views."

Halbert describes the design's impact in plain terms: "The design is spectacular. David's homes are recognizable throughout town — people know them. Zell Drive's curves and attention to detail are going to be a showstopper."

Parker's advice for whoever builds it: stay true to the vision.

> "Don't go cheap or cut corners. The result will be worth all the cost and effort."

<EditorialDivider />

## The Neighborhood

Top of the World occupies Laguna's elevated interior — panoramic views from terrain that still keeps you close to downtown and the water. Residents find it peaceful in a way the beach corridors aren't, and the community has its own loyal following among locals who know what they have.

Alta Laguna Park sits at the top of the hill, connecting to some of the finest hiking in Orange County. The Laguna Beach Unified School District serves the neighborhood. And as Halbert notes, there's something about the elevation that changes your relationship to the town below.

> "It's its own little world up here. Locals love it because it's peaceful, but still close to everything. We've got beautiful parks, hiking trails, amazing views… and there's a lot of parking!"

For the buyer who wants creative involvement in a build without the extended delays that typically precede ground-breaking in Laguna, Zell Drive is a serious opportunity.
