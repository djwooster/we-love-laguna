Lindsay DeLong's writing journey began at age seven when her physician father enlisted her help on a medical pamphlet about smoking cessation. He credited her as co-author — a document that still hangs in her office today.

Decades later, DeLong has established herself as an accomplished writer and brand strategist. Her career spans international festival reporting, ghostwriting, and sharing her personal breast cancer experience through writing and podcasting. She has built a specialty around formulating brand strategy and implementing systems and processes through web design and social media that drive business success. Her editorial experience managing over 200 creatives across writing, design, and photography roles informed her current approach.

DeLong operates Lindsay DeLong Creative, a full-service web design agency specializing in supporting women entrepreneurs. She delivers comprehensive solutions — websites, blogs, newsletters, and social media campaigns — within a two-week timeframe. For clients who have a vision but need someone to bring it to life with speed and precision, that turnaround is a game-changer.

Beyond her agency work, DeLong serves as Editor-in-Chief of Laguna Beach Living, featuring over 200 articles celebrating local businesses, entrepreneurs, and creatives. The publication posts new content twice weekly, keeping a steady pulse on the community's creative and commercial scene.
