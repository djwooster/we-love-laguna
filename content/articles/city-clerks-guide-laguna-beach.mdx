Nestled along the picturesque Southern California coastline, Laguna Beach is a vibrant city known for its stunning beaches, art festivals, and community spirit. As your trusted city clerk, I'm here to provide you with insider tips to make the most of your time in this idyllic paradise.

<EditorialDivider />

## Festival Fun for Residents

Laguna Beach residents are in for a treat with free entry to three iconic festivals — the Festival of Arts, Sawdust Festival, and Art-A-Fair. Mark your calendars for the dates when residents can enjoy these cultural celebrations without any cost. Immerse yourself in the world of art, music, and creativity surrounded by the beauty of Laguna Beach.

## Say "I Do" in Laguna Beach

For couples seeking a romantic, picturesque setting for their civil marriage ceremonies, look no further than Laguna Beach. The city clerk's office offers officiation services, including ceremonies held at historic city hall, at no cost for Laguna Beach residents and for a small fee for non-residents. Say your vows against the backdrop of this charming coastal town.

The city also offers wedding permits if you want to have your own officiant for a beach wedding at one of the city's beautiful parks or on the beach itself.

## Free Notary Services

Need documents notarized? The city clerk's office provides free notary services for one or two documents. Whether it's for legal matters or other documents, the office can assist you at no charge.

<EditorialDivider />

## Convenient Transportation

Explore Laguna Beach without worrying about parking by utilizing the city's iconic trolley or the Laguna Local on-demand neighborhood transit service. Enjoy the scenic beauty of the town without the hassle of driving.

- **Free Trolley:** The easiest way to get around — download the Trolley Tracker app
- **Laguna Local App:** On-demand, shared-ride transit between neighborhoods and activity centers; rides can also be booked by calling Transit dispatch at (949) 497-0766

## Plan Your Visit

For those new to Laguna Beach, Visit Laguna Beach is your go-to resource for comprehensive information about the city. Visit visitlagunabeach.com to discover the best attractions, dining spots, and outdoor activities in the area.

**Bonus tip:** Stay updated about upcoming events by checking lagunanow.com and tune in to Laguna's very own commercial radio station — Voice of Laguna at voiceoflaguna.com — from anywhere in the world.
