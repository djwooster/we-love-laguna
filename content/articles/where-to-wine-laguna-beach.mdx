Whether you crave sparkling bubbles, crisp whites, or deep reds, this post features five businesses serving the widest selections of wine in town! Read on to find the best wine in Laguna Beach.

## A Classy Tasting Room

The Bianchi Tasting Room is such a classy little place! I could design my dream living room around the style in there. It's a small space but very bright and welcoming. They don't currently sell cooked food, although they do have a small charcuterie plate. The Bianchi family have high standards, which is apparent in the flavors of their award-winning blends.

## A Savory Restaurant

Nirvana Grille was recently remodeled and it's a relaxing place to enjoy a glass of wine in Laguna Beach. This female owned restaurant boasts an expansive wine selection and features a savory menu with local and organic ingredients. Ask your server what wine pairs best with your meal and you won't be disappointed!

## A Unique Experience

Wine Craft is exactly what the name implies: wine and crafts! The owners of Wine Craft take pride in their finely curated selection of wines from all over the globe. While there you can try your hand at their self-guided crafts or come during a scheduled craft session and have an instructor guide you.

## A Casual Getaway

Italian inspired Laguna Canyon Winery is the first and only true winery in Orange County! Visit their Laguna Beach location where they press and ferment their grapes on site. Ask for a tour of their equipment and enjoy your wine while listening to rock & roll in their tasting room.

## A Romantic Rendezvous

For a romantic wine date, Wine Gallery is the place! They have cozy seating both indoors and out, a passionate chef serving delicious comfort foods, and as their name implies, a gallery of wine! PS – They do have beer for anybody who might not be a wine drinker.

Cheers to all you wine connoisseurs!
