Life transitions can be some of the most disorienting experiences a person faces. Whether it's a career change, a major purchase, or simply the accumulating weight of daily anxiety, the obstacles that stop us in our tracks are often rooted somewhere we can't easily reach. Susan Cavka has made it her mission to go there.

Cavka is a Rapid Transformational Therapy (RTT) practitioner who specializes in helping individuals reach their full potential by addressing the limiting beliefs stored in the subconscious since childhood.

> "RTT gets directly to the root cause of the issue. Most issues can be resolved in only one to three sessions."

## A Career Built on Getting to the Truth

Before turning to therapeutic work, Cavka spent 27 years as a private investigator in Los Angeles and Orange County. That career — built on problem-solving, pattern recognition, and intuitive reading of people — turned out to be perfect training for the work she does now. She simply redirected those skills inward, helping clients investigate their own mental landscapes.

She has personally overcome anxiety, panic attacks, OCD, fear, guilt, and limiting beliefs through RTT. That firsthand experience shapes everything about her practice.

## What RTT Can Help With

Cavka's approach addresses a wide range of challenges: stress management during major transitions like home buying or selling, change anxiety, money blocks, and coping strategies for everyday stress. For anyone navigating a significant life shift, she offers a rare combination of practical experience and deep empathy.

## On Laguna Beach

When asked what she loves about the community, Cavka highlights its sensory and creative energy.

> "I love the beauty and energy here. The homes, the ocean, and the artsy vibe."

Her favorite local activity involves exploring the beaches and tide pools at Treasure Island Beach. For newcomers, she recommends a visit to The Art of Dr. Seuss Collection at the Narrative Gallery — a whimsical reminder that imagination is always worth nurturing.
